Privacy Policy

Numfold for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch · Effective 2026-09-11

Short version: Numfold collects nothing. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no advertising. The App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”, and this page explains why that is true.

What the app stores

Your progress: stars and best times per level, Sprint and Endless points, which of today's four puzzles are done, the daily streak, and your settings (sounds, haptics, look, keypad size, game options). It is stored on the device you play on, in the app's own storage. Nobody else can read it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Sync with iCloud (optional)

If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, a copy of that progress is kept in your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud Key-Value storage, so it appears on your other iPhones, your iPad and your Apple Watch. It travels between your devices and Apple's iCloud only. We have no access to it and no way to see it. Turn the switch off at any time; the copy on each device stays. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud itself.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts. You never sign in to anything.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs, libraries or services inside the app.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No permissions requested: not notifications, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No connection to any server of ours. The only network activity is Apple's iCloud sync, and only if you turn it on.

Children

Numfold is rated 4+ and is made for adults and children alike. It has no accounts, no ads, no chat, no links out of the app and no tracking, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

App Store privacy label

The label for Numfold on the App Store is Data Not Collected. The app's privacy manifest, which Apple checks, declares no tracking and no collected data types.

This website

Static pages. No cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests it from Google's servers when a page loads; that request is covered by Google's privacy policy, and we receive nothing from it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date, and the app's privacy label will be updated to match.
