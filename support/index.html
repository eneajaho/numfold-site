How to play

Every game asks for one digit at a time on a keypad. A right tap folds into place at once. A wrong tap shows the right digit for a moment, then moves on: in Practice and Sprint it just costs time, in Endless it costs a heart.

Digit Chain

  1. Start with the first digit. Add the next digit to it.
  2. If the sum goes past 9, add its digits: 12 becomes 1 + 2 = 3.
  3. Tap the result each time. The last tap folds the whole number to one digit.
4533434: 4 + 5 = 9. 9 + 3 = 12, fold to 3. 3 + 3 = 6. 6 + 4 = 10, fold to 1. 1 + 3 = 4. 4 + 4 = 8. The answer is 8.

The keypad runs from 1 to 9. Levels grow from 3 digits to 16. In Settings › Digit Chain fold rule you can switch from Root (add the digits: 14 becomes 5) to Last digit (keep the last digit: 14 becomes 4), which adds a 0 key.

Carry

  1. Add the columns from the right, one at a time.
  2. Tap the digit that goes in the column. If the column adds to 14, tap 4.
  3. Keep the carry in your head and add it to the next column.
4787 + 2956: 7 + 6 = 13, tap 3. 8 + 5 + 1 = 14, tap 4. 7 + 9 + 1 = 17, tap 7. 4 + 2 + 1 = 7, tap 7. The sum is 7743.

Some levels are take-away, where you borrow instead of carry, and some add three numbers. Numbers grow to six digits. If holding the carry is the hard part, Settings › Carry: show the carry dot marks it for you.

Ladder

  1. A start number appears, then one operation at a time.
  2. Work each rung out in your head. The rungs disappear as you go.
  3. At the top, pick where you landed from four answers.
17, +8, ×2, −14, ÷4, +6: 25, 50, 36, 9, 15. Pick 15.

Levels grow from 3 rungs to 14. Every division is exact and the total stays between 0 and 99. Settings › Ladder pace is Slow, Normal, Fast, or Step, where each rung waits for your tap.

Balance

  1. One digit is missing from the equation.
  2. Tap the digit that makes both sides equal.
  3. The scale levels when you are right.
7 + ▢ = 15: tap 8. Later, (4 + ▢) × 3 = 27: tap 5.

The twelve levels go Add, Take away, Times, Three numbers, Brackets, Times and add, Mix, Big add, Big take away, Times to twelve, Big mix and a 12-question Marathon. The other levels have eight questions each. The missing digit is always a single digit, even when the numbers around it have two.

Modes

PracticeTwelve levels per game, three stars each, no clock. Clear a level to unlock the next. Your best time per level is kept.
SprintSixty seconds, as many steps as you can. The last five seconds tick.
EndlessThree lives; a wrong tap costs a heart, and it gets faster as you go.
Today's fourOne puzzle per game each day, the same for everyone. All four done is a perfect day. At least one a day keeps the streak.

Settings

Open Settings from the gear on the Home screen.

  • Sounds, Haptics and Big keys for a larger keypad.
  • Look: the dark look, the light look, or whatever your device is set to.
  • Digit Chain fold rule, Carry: show the carry dot and Ladder pace, described above.
  • Sync with iCloud: carries stars, best times, points and the daily streak to your iPad and Apple Watch through your own iCloud account.
  • Reset all progress: clears stars, best times and the daily streak. It asks first.

Frequently asked

Why “Numfold”?

Because every game folds a number down. A long number folds to one digit, columns fold into a sum, a ladder of operations folds into where you landed, and a scale folds level. Num, fold.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every game, every level and every mode, with no ads and no account.

How does Today's four work?

Each day the app builds one puzzle per game from the date, so everyone gets the same four. The day changes at midnight on your device. Finish all four for a perfect-day star. Play at least one and your daily streak grows by one; the streak shows until the end of the day after your last daily, so you always have a full day to keep it going.

Sounds don't play

Check that Settings › Sounds is on, then turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open. Numfold's effects play even with the Ring/Silent switch on silent and they mix with music you already have playing. If you use AirPods or another Bluetooth device, the sound goes there. Haptics are a separate switch, Settings › Haptics.

My progress isn't on my iPad or watch

Turn on Settings › Sync with iCloud on each device, and make sure they are signed in to the same iCloud account. Sync goes through Apple's iCloud and usually takes a moment; open the app on the other device once to let it pick up the change. When two devices have both played, their stars, best times, points and streak are merged, so nothing is lost.

Reset my progress

Settings › Reset all progress, then confirm. This clears stars, best times and the daily streak on that device. If Sync with iCloud is on, the reset syncs too, so your other devices follow.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later. The watch app runs on its own; it does not need the iPhone nearby.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. It is rated 4+, with no accounts, no ads, no chat and no tracking. Level one of every game uses small numbers.
